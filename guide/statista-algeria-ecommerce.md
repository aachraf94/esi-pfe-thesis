# Statista — Algeria eCommerce Market Forecast (source note)

Reference / verification note for the `statista2025` citation used in the General Introduction
(`mainmatter/introduction.tex`) and Chapter 2. Kept here so the exact figures and their
verification status are recorded in one place (Statista's own page is paywalled).

## Source

- **Title:** eCommerce — Algeria | Statista Market Forecast
- **Publisher:** Statista
- **URL:** https://www.statista.com/outlook/emo/ecommerce/algeria
- **BibTeX key:** `statista2025`

## Exact figures (as reported by Statista)

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Revenue, 2024 | **US$1,296.00m** (≈ US$1.3 billion) |
| Projected CAGR, 2024–2029 | **9.62%** (≈ 9.6%) |
| Projected market volume, 2029 | **US$2,051.00m** (exceeds US$2 billion) |

These match the figures cited in the thesis introduction (≈ US$1.3 bn in 2024, ≈ 9.6% growth,
> US$2 bn by 2029).

## Verification status (checked 2026-06)

- **Figures confirmed.** The exact values above appear in the search index from Statista's own
  page and match the thesis claims.
- **Page is paywalled.** On the live page the numbers are masked (Professional/Business account
  required), so they cannot be read directly from the public page — only via the indexed snippet.
- **No clean third-party mirror found** that reproduces Statista's exact numbers *and* credits
  Statista. Independent trackers use their own methodology and give different (generally higher)
  figures:
  - ECDB — ~US$1.72bn (2025), own methodology, does not cite Statista.
  - tunisienumerique / Mezdad — >US$1.5bn (2024), >US$2bn by 2025; cites UNCTAD, not Statista.
- All sources agree **directionally**: Algeria's e-commerce market is ~US$1.3–1.7bn now and
  heading past US$2bn within a few years. Statista's estimate is on the conservative side.

## Note

The Statista citation is correct and the figures are genuine. If a free, publicly verifiable
figure is ever preferred over a paywalled one, UNCTAD (`unctad2025`) is the stronger option — it
is already cited, authoritative, and its figures (e-commerce businesses +92%/yr since 2020; online
payments tripled 2020–2024) are publicly readable.
