## PROJECT DESCRIPTION

### Abstract

This project involves designing and deploying a Business Intelligence-oriented decision support information system for logistics management within a multi-site logistics network. The objective is to provide the company with a reliable tool for centralizing and utilizing its data from various sources (internal systems, APIs, historical data).

The system will be based on a Data Warehouse as its main foundation, fed by an ETL pipeline, and will enable the implementation of analytical dashboards and a proactive alert mechanism to support decision-makers. The targeted business areas will be prioritized:

- **Transport Requests (M - Must have):**
    - Analysis of submitted requests.
    - Assessment of associated costs.
    - Support for pricing to measure profitability.
- **Parcel Cost Control (CCC) (S - Should have):**
    - Monitoring of parcel logistics costs.
    - Support for service pricing.
    - Detection and monitoring of profitability drifts.
- **Route Analysis (C - Could have):**
    - Connection of the DW to an optimization solver (e.g., Google OR-Tools).
    - Comparison between actual routes and optimized routes.
    - Presentation of results in the form of maps and comparative KPI tables.

Thus, the project aims to provide the company with a pragmatic and evolutive BI solution, reconciling decision-making priorities and openness towards advanced modules.

**Keywords:** Business Intelligence, Data Warehouse, ETL, Dashboard, Decision Alert, Logistics, Transport Requests, Parcel Cost Control (CCC), Pricing

### Background of the Requested Work

Decision support information systems are being increasingly adopted in the logistics sector, particularly to improve operational visibility and optimize costs. Nevertheless, the majority of existing solutions remain limited: they rarely handle the integrated monitoring of transport requests, logistics cost control (Parcel Cost Control), and decision-making visualization.

In this context, the present project lies at the intersection of academic and professional needs, aiming to bring Business Intelligence practices closer to current logistical challenges, while offering a more comprehensive and scalable solution.
