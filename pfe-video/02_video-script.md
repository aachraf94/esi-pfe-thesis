# PFE Video Script (180 seconds)

> **How to read this.** Simple, clear English — read it calmly and naturally. The script is
> ~305 words, trimmed to fit 180 seconds (an earlier, longer version ran 4:03). Each block
> matches a scene in `01_claude-design-prompt.md`. Timings are a guide, not a stopwatch.
>
> The video is judged on three things: **a clear context, a clear problem, and clear
> objectives.** This script is built around exactly those three.

---

### Scene 1 — Title (0:00–0:15)

Hello. My name is Achraf Abdelkebir, a final-year student at ESI.
My project is titled
*Decision Support Information System for Logistics Operations and Cost Analysis*.
It was supervised by Mr. Abbas Mohamed Amir at ESI, and Mrs. Rihane Yasmine at Ourquilane —
the company where I carried out the project.

---

### Scene 2 — Context (0:15–0:40)

Today, logistics companies produce huge amounts of data —
every parcel, trip, and cost leaves a digital trace.
In Algeria, online shopping is booming, and delivery companies must grow while controlling
costs. Yet most cannot really use this data — they react to problems instead of
anticipating them.

---

### Scene 3 — The players (0:40–0:55)

This project brings together two partners.
**Ourquilane**, the Algerian software company that builds IT solutions,
and **Yalidine Express**, the client — Algeria's express-delivery leader,
with a nationwide network of hubs, centres, and agencies.

---

### Scene 4 — The problem (0:55–1:20)

Now, the problem.
Yalidine generates a lot of data — parcels, transport, expenses, salaries — spread across
separate systems that do not talk to each other.

So when a manager asks —
- *How are we performing?*
- *What does it cost?*
- *Are we profitable?*

— there is no easy answer.
The data exists, but it is scattered, slow, and hard to trust.

---

### Scene 5 — The question (1:20–1:30)

So the main question is simple:
how can we turn this scattered data into reliable, useful decisions?

---

### Scene 6 — The solution (1:30–1:45)

My answer is a Business Intelligence system.
It collects data from every source system, cleans it automatically, stores it in one
central place, and turns it into clear dashboards and alerts.

---

### Scene 7 — Objectives (1:45–2:20)

The project has four objectives.

1. **First**, bring all the scattered data into one single, reliable source.
2. **Second**, give a deep reading of each activity.
3. **Third**, monitor proactively — warn the team early when a key indicator drifts.
4. **And fourth**, give each decision-maker the right view, so choices rest on evidence, not
   on intuition.

---

### Scene 8 — What it covers (2:20–2:40)

The system covers two activities: **dedicated transport** and **parcel delivery**.

For each, it covers three areas:
- operations,
- cost and profitability,
- and performance.

---

### Scene 9 — The outcome (2:40–2:55)

The result is a real change.
Instead of searching many systems by hand, managers open one screen and see a clear,
trustworthy picture of the whole business — and act before problems grow.

---

### Scene 10 — Closing (2:55–3:00)

In short: scattered logistics data, turned into clear, reliable decisions.
Thank you.

---

> **Word count ≈ 305** (≈ 180 s at your trial pace). If you still run long, the safest extra
> cuts are the second halves of Scene 2 and Scene 4. Do not cut Scene 7 — the objectives are
> the most important part.
