## THE PROMPT (copy everything below)

You are designing a **180-second presentation video** for a Final Year Project (PFE)
**pre-defense jury check** at ESI (École Nationale Supérieure d'Informatique, Algiers).
This video is reviewed by the jury before the defense to confirm the student can clearly
identify a context, a problem, and the project objectives. I have attached three
logos: **ESI**, **Ourquilane**, and **Yalidine Express**. Use them exactly as attached —
do not redraw or recolour them.

### Project in one line
A **Business Intelligence (BI) decision-support system** that turns a logistics company's
scattered data into clear, reliable dashboards and proactive alerts. **Do not show any
product / solution name anywhere in the video** — refer to it only as "the system" or
"the solution".

### Deliverable
A clean, modern **16:9 slide deck / motion presentation** (10 scenes, ~180 seconds total)
that plays behind a voice-over. Design every scene to be readable in 2–3 seconds: big
visuals, very little text, no paragraphs on screen.

### Brand & visual style
- **Primary colour:** ESI blue `RGB(0, 84, 166)` (hex `#0054A6`). Use it for accents,
  headings, and key shapes.
- **Supporting palette:** white background, dark-grey text (`#222`), one warm accent
  (amber/orange) reserved only for "alert" / "problem" moments.
- **Typography:** one clean sans-serif (e.g. Inter, Helvetica, or Arial). Large headlines,
  generous white space.
- **Tone:** professional, calm, confident, slightly technical — an engineering-school
  defense, not a flashy ad. Smooth, simple transitions (fades, soft slides). No gimmicks.
- **Iconography:** simple flat line icons (data, warehouse, pipeline, dashboard, bell/alert,
  map of Algeria, parcel/truck).
- **Logos:** show all three together on the title and closing scenes. Where a single
  organisation is mentioned in a scene, you may show its logo small in a corner.

### Scene-by-scene storyboard (match the script timing)

**Scene 1 — Title (0:00–0:20).**
Academic title card (no product name). The PFE title, large and centred:
*"Decision Support Information System for Logistics Operations and Cost Analysis"*.
Below it, the author **ABDELKEBIR Achraf**, and the supervisors *Mr. ABBAS Mohamed Amir
(ESI)* and *Mrs. RIHANE Yasmine (Ourquilane)*. The three logos (ESI · Ourquilane ·
Yalidine Express) sitting together along the bottom. Clean, centred, confident.

**Scene 2 — Context: the data age in logistics (0:15–0:40).**
A world of moving parcels and trucks; lots of small data points / records streaming in.
Convey "huge volumes of data generated every day". End the scene zooming into a map of
**Algeria** with a rising e-commerce / growth arrow. One stat on screen at a time, large:
e.g. *"e-commerce in Algeria growing fast"*, *"only 7% of supply chains act on live data"*.

**Scene 3 — Meet the players (0:40–0:55).**
Introduce the companies with their logos: **Ourquilane** (the software company that built
the system) and **Yalidine Express** (the client — Algeria's express-delivery leader,
nationwide network). Show a simple national network: hubs → regional centres → delivery
agencies across Algeria.

**Scene 4 — The problem: scattered data (0:55–1:20).**
Visual of **several disconnected systems / boxes** that do not talk to each other (parcels,
transport, expenses, payroll, HR). Show a confused decision-maker who cannot get a clear
answer. Use the warm accent colour here. On-screen idea: *"The data exists — but it's
fragmented, slow, and manual."* This is the emotional low point of the video.

**Scene 5 — The driving question (1:20–1:30).**
A single clean text scene, large and centred:
*"How can we turn scattered logistics data into reliable, proactive decisions?"*

**Scene 6 — The solution overview (1:30–1:45).**
Reveal **the system** as the answer (no product name on screen). Show a simple
left-to-right pipeline:
**Source systems → ETL pipeline → Data Warehouse → Dashboards → Alerts.** Animate data
flowing along it once. This is the "aha" turn — bring back ESI blue, bright and confident.

**Scene 7 — The four objectives (1:45–2:20).**
Four clean icon cards appearing one by one (these are goals, not technical components):
1. **One reliable source** — all the scattered data brought together
2. **A deep reading of each activity** — clear analytics per activity
3. **Proactive monitoring** — early warning when an indicator drifts
4. **The right view for each decision-maker** — decide on evidence, not intuition
Keep one short label per card. This is the core of the video — give it room.

**Scene 8 — Two business axes & three views (2:20–2:40).**
Show the two analysis axes side by side: **Dedicated Transport** and **Parcel Delivery**.
Under each, three small tabs: **Operations · Cost & Profitability · Performance**. Suggest
real dashboard look — KPI cards, a line chart, a bar chart, a map of Algeria. (Generic /
mock charts; no real numbers needed.)

**Scene 9 — The outcome / value (2:40–2:55).**
A confident decision-maker now sees one clear dashboard. Convey: *"From fragmented data to
one reliable, decision-ready view."* Calm, resolved, ESI-blue.

**Scene 10 — Closing (2:55–3:00).**
Closing card (no product name): the PFE title, author **ABDELKEBIR Achraf**, ESI option
*Information Systems and Technologies (SIT)*, year *2025/2026*, supervisors *Mr. ABBAS
Mohamed Amir (ESI)* and *Mrs. RIHANE Yasmine (Ourquilane)*. The three logos together.
Simple "Thank you".

### Important constraints
- **Minimal on-screen text** — the voice-over carries the words; slides carry the visuals.
- Never put more than one short sentence (or a few labels) on a single scene.
- Keep it strictly **16:9**, readable on a projector from the back of a room.
- Stay consistent: same fonts, same colours, same icon style throughout.
- Deliver the scenes in order, labelled Scene 1–10, so they can be timed to the script.
