\chapter{Data Warehouse Reference}
\label{anx:dw}

This annexe complements the conceptual design of \S\ref{sec:ch4-dw} with the warehouse's reference
detail: the complete table inventory, the field-level data dictionary of the fact tables and the core
dimensions, and the full constellation ERD. Field types are given generically (the physical
realisation is in Chapter~5).
